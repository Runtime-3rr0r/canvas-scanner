# Email Drafting

## Email drafting (<USER_NAME>'s style — learn from every sent draft)

There is NO reliable "type into Outlook compose" automation (the preview-pane compose field mapping is broken — typing got routed to To/Cc/body wrongly, twice). **Always deliver emails as text in chat** for copy-paste; reading Outlook (Sent folder) for diffing IS reliable.

<USER_NAME>'s style fingerprint (learned from his sent Chem-lecture-miss apology 9/9/26 — update as he edits more):
- Apologize directly first, zero preamble ("I apologize for..." not "I'm writing to apologize for...")
- Short declarative sentences, one idea each
- Own the mistake fully — NO defensive hedging. But: do NOT be overly apologetic or personal; stay professional and brief (competent student, not begging)
- **NO em dashes — ever.** They read as AI-generated. Use commas/periods instead.
- Write for a human, human-sounding voice: natural, approachable, not stiff. Easier to build trust.
- End forward-positive: next steps / readiness, not deficit ("so I don't fall behind and am ready for next week's content")
- Close: "Best," + full name + student ID (<STUDENT_ID>)
- Concise — every sentence earns its place
- Professor email subject: COURSE-SECTION: topic (e.g. "<COURSE_CODE>-B: Missed September 9 Lecture")

## Inbox catch-up ("catch me up on mail")

Reliable: open `https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/mail/inbox`, read the sidebar (desktop_preview read) to enumerate unread + previews. The MAIL READING PANE is flaky for sequential click-through: it re-pins to the first opened message and refs go stale (same compose/pane bug class).
